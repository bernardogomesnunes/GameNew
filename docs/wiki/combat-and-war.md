# Combat and war

## Fighting

- Break (left click) attacks. Swords hit hardest.
- Blows vary. About one in six is heavy: twice as hard, and it throws the target back.
- Drinks help: Beer for quicker blows, Kombucha for +2 on every hit, Coffee for speed.
- Temple upgrades add Thunder (stun), Fire (burn) and Ice (freeze) to a Stone or Iron Sword.
- Armour cuts damage. See [Items](items.md#armour).
- The Black Ring sparks back at whoever hits you.
- You have ten hearts. Dying leaves a chest where you fell with what you carried.

### Bow and arrows

1. Grow hemp. Make Hemp Fibre into String (3 fibre makes 1 string).
2. Make a Bow (3 Planks, 3 String) and Arrows (1 Plank, 1 String makes 4). Both at Age 1.
3. Hold Break to draw. A full draw takes 0.9 seconds. The longer you draw, the faster and harder the arrow flies.
4. A twitch, tabbing away or pausing shoots nothing.
5. Arrows drop a little over distance, hit animals and bandits for 5, and stick in blocks. They go back into your bag if you walk over them within 60 seconds.
6. Arrows hit everything in the dark army, rams and catapults included.

### Catapult

- Make one: 10 Planks, 4 Wood, 6 Stone (Age 4).
- Place on it to man it. Look where you want the stone to land. An arc and a ring show where it will fall.
- Break throws a stone from your bag. It lobs at 50 degrees, or 70 to clear a wall.
- A stone knocks out a crater and hurts bandits and animals under it. Your claimed buildings, chests and bedrock are spared.

## Bandits and raiders

- Hostile from Age 2. They come at you near their camp, further out at night, hit for a heart, and run when badly hurt.
- Most nights, 2 to 3 raiders walk in from the nearest camp to rob a storehouse, or come for you if you have none.
- Beat a raider and you get back what they took.

## Soldiers

Build a **Barracks**. A new one holds 10 soldiers; evolve it for 20, then 40. A **Garrison** works the same way for spear guards (10, 20, 40). Pick who to train from its pop-up and pay up front from your bag. They join one at a time, about 2 minutes 15 seconds each, and live in town.

| Soldier | Cost | Health | Fights |
|---|---|---|---|
| Warrior | 3 food, a Stone Sword | 20 | Melee, 3 per blow, every 1.1 seconds |
| Swordsman | 4 food, an Iron Sword, an Iron Cuirass | 32 | Melee, 5 per blow, every second |
| Archer | 3 food, a Bow, 20 Arrows | 14 | Arrow up to 20 blocks, 3 damage, every 2 seconds |
| Catapult crew | 5 food, a Catapult, 10 Stone | 16 | Sets up a catapult in front of the barracks and lobs stones up to 38 blocks |

- A fallen soldier is gone. Training another costs the same again.
- Hungry soldiers hit at 60% until fed.
- A bar on your screen shows who is nearest to finishing training.

## Defence buildings (Age 8)

| Building | What it is |
|---|---|
| Stone Wall | A section at least 8 long and 4 tall, up to 4 thick, with a walk along the top behind battlements. Each block takes three blows from a ram or siege stone to break |
| Gatehouse | Two towers with guardrooms, a gate of at least two doors, battlements. It shuts its gate before a round |
| Watchtower | At least 11 tall, a stair up inside, a lantern on top, battlements. Two archers keep watch and shoot anything of the Stone Kingdom's within 26 blocks |
| Barracks | 2 weapon racks, 2 training dummies or archery targets, and a hall bigger than a house |

Raiders break blocks for real. A claimed building that loses blocks stops working until you put them back.

## The war: the Ten Rounds

The war begins the moment you reach **Age 10**, whichever ring you bear. The wall comes down and the Stone Kingdom declares war.

- The game cannot be finished while the war is on.
- The first round comes half a game day after the declaration. Rounds come 1.5 game days apart.
- A war horn sounds about a minute of play before each round, saying how many are coming and from where.
- You can sound your own **War Horn** (2 Hide, 1 Gold, 1 Planks; Age 10) to call the next round early.
- A round you lose comes again a day later. There is no game over. Raiders loot a share of your storehouses.
- A round you win pays gold: 6 for round 1, up to 24 for round 10.
- Forge the **Black Ring** and the Stone King calls his army home. The war is off. That is the dark path.

| 3 | 5 bandits |

Win round 10 and the raids end for good. Fireflies drift across your land at night, and the white ending plays. See [Lore and story](lore.md).

## The god's cube

After the war is won (white path) or the Sky Kingdom has fallen (dark path), you can step through from your Sanctuary to fight the god you did not choose, or steal his stash. Full details are in [Lore and story](lore.md#the-gods-cube-the-last-fight).

## Guardians

Raise a Sanctuary and your god's guardian comes to you. It fights bandits and follows your orders: tap it to cycle follow, stay, attack.

| | Aurelion (white) | Umbra (black) |
|---|---|---|
| Looks like | A great stag of light | A beast of shadow |
| Health | 60 | 70 |
| Gift | Heals you while you are near it | Enemies near it lose their nerve and run |

If it is downed, it goes back to its Sanctuary and returns after a day.

## The dark path's army

After you swear the oath, the Stone King gives you **1,000 warriors**. About 30 fight on the ground at once; when one falls, the count drops and a fresh one marches in.

- **Command wheel:** tap the sword banner on your screen. Orders: follow, hold here, attack, form a line.
- **Food:** every game day the army eats a meal for every hundred warriors, from your bag then your storehouses. Short of food, 3% desert each day.
- **War Tent** (4 Wool, 2 Planks): wake there, and the army holds round it.
- **Campfire** (2 Planks, 2 Stone): burns with a light of its own.
- **Expedition:** from the map, send the army to the Sky Kingdom. It marches on its own for 2 days and makes camp beyond the nearest anchor tower: a cleared patch, three war tents, a campfire and two black banners. Use "Join your army at its camp" to go there. Your first order breaks camp. While it is away nobody is with you on the ground.

## Battle worlds

Choose Battle when creating a world to add three rival computer-controlled cities. They grow in strength as game days pass. Your first working Barracks starts their attack schedule: the first army is due after half a game day, and the others follow at staggered intervals. A warning precedes an attack. Only one rival army fights at a time.

Beat an army to earn Gold and weaken its city. A defeat strengthens it. Enter a rival city, defeat its garrison and use its War Standard to claim it. That halves the rival's strength, pays tribute, and stops its armies for three game days while it rebuilds. There is no permanent final victory over all rivals; conquered cities can recover. Battle state saves with the world.

These rivals run in the game. API-driven rivals and real multiplayer are not part of this implementation.
