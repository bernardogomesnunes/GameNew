# Getting started

## Your first hour

You land on a plot 32 blocks wide with an axe, a bucket, 4 fruit, 4 carrot seeds and 4 potato seeds. You cannot walk past the edge of your land until Age 10.

1. **Chop a tree.** Aim at a trunk and hold Break (left click). You get wood.
2. **Fill your bucket.** Hold the bucket and tap water.
3. **Make a pickaxe.** Open the Workbench (E). A Stone Pickaxe needs 3 Wood and 4 Stone. Stone comes from boulders near the start, or from cobblestone, white stone or marble ("Stone from ..." recipes).
4. Your land grows to 64 blocks. Now **claim a forest**, **a farm** and **a house**. Open the Buildings panel (B) to place ready-made ones, or build your own and press C to name it.
5. Eat before the hunger bar drops under 35 of 100, or you slow down. Hunt deer, rabbits and boar. Cook raw meat for six times the food.
6. Build a **second house**. A settler moves in. Settlers work your buildings and eat from your storehouses.
7. Hang a **Painting** in your house and Place on it. That is where you wake if you fall.
8. Keep going: each age asks for three or four buildings. See [Goals](goals.md).

## Villagers can help

Build a Work Hall from the Buildings list when you have villagers. Assign Builders for queued construction or Haulers to move the supplies you leave at the hall. Make sure they have food and a clear walking route.

## Tips

- Buildings send what they make to your storehouses first, then your bag.
- Build workplaces within 40 blocks of your houses, or settlers will not walk to them.
- Evolve buildings (lamps, then chests, then coins and iron) for up to 3.5 times the output.
- Keep spare mining tools. Carpentry tools and the Weapon Mold are reusable.
- Light a lantern or two. Night lasts 5 real minutes.
- Cook your meat. Raw meat feeds 5. Cooked feeds 32.

## Game modes

| | Duilt (recommended) | Creative |
|---|---|---|
| Start | 32 blocks, an axe, a bucket, seeds | Generated terrain, every block available |
| Progress | Twelve ages, goals, quests | None |
| Border | Walls you in until Age 10 | None |
| Costs | Placing and claiming cost items | Free |
| Bag | 40 slots, items run out | One of everything, never runs out |
| Hunger and damage | Yes | Never hungry, never hurt |
| Buildings | Must be built and claimed | Work without cost |
| Recipes | Gated by age | All open |
| Story | Intro, tales, lore, war, two paths | Skipped, but the Sky Kingdom and Stone Kingdom are in the world to visit |

You can save worlds in your browser, export them to a file, or sync them to your account.

Battle worlds add three rival computer cities. Their armies begin after your first Barracks; see [Battle worlds](combat-and-war.md#battle-worlds).

## Controls

### Keyboard and mouse

All movement keys can be changed in Settings, Controls and sound.

| Action | Default |
|---|---|
| Walk | W, A, S, D |
| Jump, swim up, fly up | Space |
| Run, or fly faster | Left Ctrl (or double-tap forward) |
| Sneak, swim down, fly down | Left Shift |
| Fly on and off | F |
| Turn a roof or saved design | R |
| Change view (your eyes, behind, in front) | F5 |
| Break, attack, shoot | Left click |
| Place, open, use, ride, drive | Right click |
| Hotbar slot | 1 to 9 |

### Panels

| Panel | Key | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Goals (also Lore, Quests, Today, Book) | G | Your age's goals and everything you have learned |
| Map | M | Places you have found |
| Bag | I | 40 slots plus the Wearing row |
| Workbench | E | Everything you can make, with search |
| Skills | K | Your four skills |
| Claim ("What is this?") | C | Point at something you built and name it |
| Buildings | B | Place a ready-made building |

Other screens: Settings (Notifications, World details, Graphics, Controls and sound, Files), Designs (save and stamp your own builds), Roofs, Clear, and each trader's stall.

### Phones and tablets

- Left thumb: joystick to walk. Drag anywhere else to look.
- Tap the world to break, attack or fill a bucket.
- Place and Jump are on the right. You can swap sides in Settings.

### Bag and hotbar

- The first 9 bag slots are the hotbar.
- The Wearing row holds head, body, legs, feet, back (backpacks) and ring.
- Tap an item to lift it, tap a slot to put it down, hold to split a stack.
- Tools show a bar on the hotbar: green, amber, then red as they wear out.
- Press F5 to see yourself in third person. You can change how your character looks in Settings.
