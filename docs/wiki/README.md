# Duilt Wiki

Everything in the game, in lists. Look up an item, a building, a goal, a creature or a piece of the story.

Duilt is a settlement game in a world of blocks. You are an exile from a kingdom that floats in the sky. You start on a small plot of land, build a settlement, and push back the border through twelve ages. Two kingdoms and two gods decide how your story ends.

## The game in one minute

1. **Age 1 to 3:** chop wood, fetch water, make tools, claim a forest, a farm and a house, then a quarry and a storehouse.
2. **Age 4 to 6:** workshop, kiln, animals, a market, tavern and foundry, a town hall, a university.
3. **Age 7 to 9:** a temple where you choose a god by forging a ring, a mine, a barracks, walls.
4. **Age 10:** the border falls. The Stone Kingdom attacks in ten rounds. Or, if you forged the Black Ring, you take the Sky Kingdom instead.
5. **After the war:** step through to the god's cube and face the god you did not choose.
6. **Age 11 and 12:** explore the old world, trade, collect everything, travel through time, and finish the game.
