# Buildings

A **building** is a part of the world the game agrees to call something. You make one in one of two ways:

1. **Build it yourself and claim it.** Build the thing, point at it, press **C**, and choose what it is. If something is missing, the panel tells you exactly what ("Needs 12 wood").
2. **Place a ready-made one.** Press **B**, pick a design, and it builds in front of you for the cost of its materials.

Once claimed, a building is locked. You can unlock it to edit the blocks, or give up the claim.

A claimed building:

- makes things on a timer and sends them to your storehouses first, then your bag;
- makes 50% more when a settler works there;
- can be **evolved** through levels by giving it what the next level needs and paying its cost.

## Levels

Most buildings that make things share one ladder:

| Level | Name | Costs | Also needs | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Humble | nothing | nothing | normal |
| 1 | Tended | 12 Planks, 8 Cobblestone | 1 lamp | 1.5 times |
| 2 | Thriving | 20 Planks, 15 Coins | 2 lamps, 1 chest | 2.5 times |
| 3 | Renowned | 4 Iron Ingot, 40 Coins | 4 lamps, 2 chests | 3.5 times |

Other ladders:

- **Quarry:** A Scrape, A Working Face, A Deep Dig, A Quarry Face, An Open Pit. It rises as you dig more of the rock out.
- **Storehouse:** Shelves (200 slots), Loft (240), Warehouse (280).
- **Temple:** Shrine, Chapel, Temple, Great Temple, High Temple. Each is paid for in devotion.
- **Market:** every level brings one more trader to live in it (see [Trading](trading.md)).

Tips for evolving:

- You can build the lamps and chests in yourself, or press Evolve and take them from your bag.
- **Craft what's missing** (the button beside Evolve) makes whatever the next level lacks from what is in your bag.
- Lights placed very close together count as one.

## Settlers and houses

- A House holds one household. A Townhouse and a Village hold three each.
- The first house you build is your own. Each house after that brings a settler household.
- Settlers walk up to 40 blocks to work.

## Building details

Cycle times are in real minutes of play.


### Forest

Standing trees that keep giving, instead of a stump you cut once.

- Unlocks at age 1 (Exile).
- Size: 4 to 16 blocks on a side.
- Makes: 5 Wood, 1 Leaves, 1 Fruit every 18.8 minutes at the first level (more at higher levels).

What it needs:

- Needs 12 wood, grow or plant more trees
- Needs 20 leaves, the trees are too bare
- Needs open soil between the trees

Levels (press Evolve in the building's pop-up):

| Level | Name | Costs | Also needs | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Humble Forest | - | - | ×1 of the first level |

| 3 | Renowned Forest | 4 Iron Ingot, 40 Coins | 4 lights; 2 chests | 18 Wood, 4 Leaves, 4 Fruit |

- Dig down until 24 stone blocks show inside it

- Open it to the sky, it can’t be underground

### Stone Wall

A length of wall with a walk along the top and battlements to fight from. Claimed, it takes three blows a block to break.

- Unlocks at age 8 (Bastion).
- Size: 2 to 32 blocks on a side.
- A defence building for the war.

What it needs:

- A wall needs to run at least 8 blocks, this one is 6
- A wall is long and narrow, no more than 4 blocks thick. Claim it along its length.
- Needs to stand at least 4 blocks tall, yours is 4
- Needs 30 stone, cobble, brick or dark stone, a wall has to be solid
- Needs battlements along the top, stones with gaps between them, to fight from behind

### Gatehouse

Two towers and a gate between them, with a walk across the top. It shuts its gate when a round of the war is coming.

- Unlocks at age 8 (Bastion).
- Size: 7 to 16 blocks on a side.
- Claim cost: 2 Iron Ingot.
- A defence building for the war.

What it needs:

- Needs 120 stone, cobble, brick or dark stone, a gatehouse is the strongest part of a wall
- Its towers need to stand at least 8 blocks tall, yours is 4
- Needs a gate, at least two doors side by side (0 so far)
- Needs a guardroom in its towers, walls all round and a floor over it
- Needs battlements along the top, to fight from

### Watchtower

- Unlocks at age 8 (Bastion).
- Size: 5 to 9 blocks on a side.

What it needs:

- Needs to stand at least 11 blocks tall to see over the land, yours is 4
- Needs 70 stone, cobble, brick or dark stone
- Needs a stair up inside it, 6 stairs
- Needs a lantern on top, a signal light for the watch
- Needs battlements round the top, for the archers to shoot from behind

### Barracks
