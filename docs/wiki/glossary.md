# Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Age | One of twelve steps. Each has a land size and a few goals |
| Anchor tower | One of four towers on the ground that hold the Sky Kingdom's chains |
| Bag | Your inventory. Forty slots. The first nine are the hotbar |
| Break | The left-click action. Digs, chops, attacks, shoots |
| Cell | One block position in the world |
| Claim | Telling the game "this is a farm". Needs the right blocks inside |
| Coins | Money. Struck at a foundry, taken by traders |
| Craft what's missing | A button beside Evolve that makes whatever the next level lacks |
| Devotion | Resource made at a Temple from worshippers and offerings |
| Evolve | Raise a building to its next level |
| Game day | 15 real minutes |
| Goal | A requirement of an age. Finishing all of an age's goals moves the border out |
| Guardian | The beast your god sends when you raise a Sanctuary |
| Hotbar | The first nine bag slots, selected with keys 1 to 9 |
| Humble, Tended, Thriving, Renowned | The four levels of most buildings |
| Landmark | A place made by the world, such as a hut, camp, ruin or city |
| Lord of the Sky | Your title when you take the Sky Kingdom |
| Place | The right-click action. Sets blocks, and also opens, uses, rides, drives |
| Ring | The White or Black Ring. Forged once per world |
| Round | One wave of the Stone Kingdom's attack. There are ten |
| Sneak | Move lower and slowly. Also gets you out of a vehicle |
| Station | A building or block where certain recipes can be made |
| Tier | A material grade of a tool, or a level of a building |
| Tale | A line of story told by a messenger or the hermit |
| Warrior | One of the 1,000 given by the Stone King (or the cheap barracks soldier) |
| Workbench | The crafting panel (E) |
