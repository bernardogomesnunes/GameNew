# The world

## Biomes

Ten kinds of country. Similar ones sit together.

| Biome | What it is like |
|---|---|
| Plains | Open grass, few trees, huge flat patches. Your settlement starts here. Wild hemp grows here. Horses, sheep, cows, pigs and chickens |
| Oak Forest | The everyday wood on moss. Dense trees |
| Birch Forest | Cooler. Pale trunks and leaves. Grass underfoot |
| Dark Forest | The thickest, darkest wood on dark moss. Boar |
| Giant Grove | Rare. Gigantic trees on a floor of fallen leaves. Tree tribes live here |
| Desert | Sand over sandstone, with rocky outcrops. Rabbits |
| Wetland | Low flat clay ground with pools |
| Mountains | A short range about 20 blocks tall, with streams. Bare gravel above the tree line. Goats |
| High Peaks | Rare and very tall (50 to 70 blocks). Most of the ore is here |
| Ocean | Deep water ringed by sand beaches |

## Resources

| Resource | Where |
|---|---|
| Wood | Trees. Oak in most places, white wood in birch forests, dark wood in dark forests |
| Stone | Boulders near the start, any rock layer, quarries |
| White, dark grey, turquoise and orange stone | Rock layers. Orange is the rarest |
| Gravel | Mountains, and pockets in the rock at any depth (about 4 blocks in 100) |
| Sand, clay | Riverbanks, beaches, deserts, wetland |
| Sandstone | Three blocks under desert sand, and in outcrops |
| Iron, copper, gold | High Peaks (iron 1.8%, copper 1.4%, gold 0.8% of the rock) |
| Coal | High Peaks (2.4% of the rock, the commonest ore). Also mines and charcoal |
| Ruby | High Peaks (0.4%). A foundry strikes 25 coins from one |
| Diamond | Deepest rock, near the bottom of the world. Rare |
| Obsidian, amethyst | Deep underground |
| Sunstone, Nightstone | Very rare crystals on cave walls deep down, and in chests. Needed for rings |
| Lava | Caverns |
| Fruit, saplings | Forests. Breaking leaves gives a sapling (10%) or fruit (6%) |
| Seeds | Breaking grass (about 1 in 5) |
| Wild hemp | Plains and birch forests |
| Berry thickets | By water near the start |

## Places to find

Distances are in blocks from the middle of the world. Each place is placed from the world's seed, so the same seed always has the same places.

| Place | How many | How far | What is there |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hermit's hut | 1 | 320 to 420 | The hermit, and a chest |
| Bandit camp | 3 | 480 to 720 | Bandits and a chest of takings |
| Ruin | 4 | 300 to 700 | Broken walls and pillars, a chest |
| Abandoned mine | 2 | 300 to 850 | Timber over a hole, a chest |
| Monument | 2 | 380 to 900 | A tall black stone, gold at the top, a chest |
| Forgotten temple | 2 | 450 to 950 | Marble pillars, an altar, a chest |
| Tree tribe | 3 | 380 to 950 | A village in giant trees. They never come down |
| Living city | 2 | 480 to 1,050 | Walls, guards, plaza, merchants, a dozen houses, a train station |
| Abandoned city | 2 | 480 to 1,050 | The same, crumbled and empty, with more and better chests |
| Stone Kingdom outpost | 2 | 600 to 1,150 | A dark-brick fort, four guards, an armoury of two chests |
| The Stone Kingdom | 1 | 1,000 to 1,400 | The walled dark city |
| The Sky Kingdom | 1 | about 5,000 | The floating island (dark path, and Creative) |

- Old roads join every landmark to home. A road always leads to the Stone Kingdom's gate.
- Come within about 24 blocks of a place to find it. You get a message and a marker on the map.
- Messengers and explorers tell you of places, with a direction.
- Anything you build in a distant chunk shows up in the far view.
- The Stone Kingdom's walls show on the horizon. The Sky Kingdom shows as a gold light low in the sky, brighter at night.
- Cities and the Stone Kingdom's outposts are far out, past the old border.

### Special places in detail

**Tree tribes.** Villages in the tallest trees: decks, bridges, huts under the leaves, a stair up to a watchtower on the crown. The people never come down. They are neutral until struck or robbed.

**Living cities.** A walled square with a gate north and south, a cross of calçada streets, a plaza with a statue, and two merchants with goods your market does not stock. A dozen houses, each lit. Each city is a train station and has its own name.

**Abandoned cities.** The same plan, gone to ruin: walls fallen to stumps, roofs caved in, moss and gravel in the streets, nobody home, and better chests.

**Stone Kingdom outposts.** A fort of dark brick with four of the Kingdom's guards, and an armoury of two chests (diamonds, rubies, dark armour, coins). They treat you as the Kingdom does, and come for you if you rob the armoury.

## Chests and loot

What is in a chest is fixed when it is first opened, so the same chest always holds the same things. Every kind has a small chance of a ring crystal (Sunstone or Nightstone).

| Chest | Typical contents |
|---|---|
| Old cave chest | Gold, iron, copper, cooked meat, lanterns, coffee, coins |
| Bandits' takings | Gold, meat, iron, wool, beer, kombucha, coins, rarely a stone sword |
| Hermit's chest | Fruit, seeds, eggs, lanterns, gold, kombucha, coffee beans |
| Ruin chest | Cooked meat, fruit, a stone sword, leather armour, beer, gold, coins |
| Abandoned mine chest | Iron, copper, gold, coffee, dark armour, leather boots, meat, coins |
| Monument offering | Gold, iron sword, dark and Sky armour, drinks, fruit; rarely an upgraded piece |
| Forgotten temple offering | Gold, holy water, devotion, an iron sword, Sky armour; rarely an upgraded piece |

| City house chest | Meat, wool, gold, beer, lanterns, a painting, coins |
| Abandoned city chest | Gold, gold sword and armour, iron, amethyst, gold trim, a painting, rubies, diamonds, coins |
| Stone Kingdom chest | Iron, dark stone, gold, iron sword, dark armour, beer, coins |
| Outpost armoury | Iron, dark stone, gold, dark sword, diamonds, rubies, dark armour, arrows, 20 to 50 coins |

## How the world changes

- Leaves rot away when their tree is gone.
- Water flows. A bucket clears a lone water block; water joined to other water stays.
- Turf spreads onto bare dirt over 5 to 50 game days.
- Night falls twice as fast as day. Fireflies come out, and lanterns matter.
- Animals, messengers and explorers come and go.
