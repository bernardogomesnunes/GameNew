### Crops

Nine plants grow on farmland, each in four stages of 90 seconds. They are ripe in about four and a half minutes.

| Crop | Gives | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Carrot | Carrots | Starting seed |
| Potato | Potatoes | Starting seed. Brewed into beer |
| Cabbage | Cabbage | |
| Lettuce | Lettuce | |
| Pepper | Peppers | |
| Zucchini | Zucchini | |
| Broccoli | Broccoli | |
| Coffee | Coffee Beans | Seeds are in the hermit's chest, from traders, and from quests |
| Hemp | Hemp Fibre | Not food. Grows wild on open grass. Fibre makes string, string makes bows |

- Break a ripe plant for its crop and seeds. Break it early and you get its seed back.
- Breaking grass sometimes drops seeds (about 1 in 5).
- A farm takes one seed per crop, up to four crops.
- You can plant and pick by hand in a farm's soil. What you plant there is yours.
- A sapling grows into a tree in about 10 game days.

## Armour

You wear head, body, legs, feet, back and ring.

- Each point of armour takes 4% off a blow from a bandit, a catapult stone, the Stone Kingdom's army or the Sky Kingdom's guards.
- A blow never drops below half a heart.
- Armour does nothing against falls or lava.
- Every blow wears it down.
- A full Sky or Dark set (13 points) takes about half off. A full Diamond set (17 points) takes about two thirds.
- A full Sky set lets you pass for a Sky Kingdom native on the island (the dark path's disguise).
- Gold armour turns blows better than iron but wears faster.
- Taking off a backpack moves its contents into the rest of the bag. If there is no room, it stays on and tells you how much to clear.


| Set | Unlocks | Helm | Body | Legs | Boots | Total | Uses | Made from |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Leather | Age 1 | 1 | 2 | 1 | 1 | 5 | 80 | Stitched from hides |
| Iron | Age 5 | 2 | 3 | 2 | 1 | 8 | 200 | Plate from your foundry |
| Gold | Age 8 | 2 | 4 | 2 | 1 | 9 | 160 | Beaten gold, it turns a blow better than iron, and wears faster |
| Diamond | Age 10 | 4 | 6 | 4 | 3 | 17 | 600 | Diamond, bound in iron, the best armour there is |
| Sky | Age 5 | 3 | 5 | 3 | 2 | 13 | 300 | White steel and gold, as the Sky Kingdom wears it |
| Dark | Age 5 | 3 | 5 | 3 | 2 | 13 | 300 | Blackened iron, as the Stone Kingdom wears it |


Each set has four pieces: Helm, Cuirass, Greaves and Boots. Leather is Cap, Tunic, Leggings and Boots. Backpacks go in the Back slot: Leather (+10 slots) and Reinforced (+20 slots).

The **Collector's Crown** is a reward from the Collection Book. It is a head piece with 5 points that never wears out.
