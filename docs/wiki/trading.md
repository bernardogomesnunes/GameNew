# Trading

Traders take **coins** only. Get coins by striking gold, copper or rubies at a Foundry, by running a Town Hall, by selling to traders, or from chests.

| Coin recipe (Foundry) | Gives |
|---|---|
| 1 Gold | 10 coins |
| 1 Copper Ingot | 3 copper coins |
| 1 Ruby | 25 coins |

## Market traders

Build a Market (Age 5) and its first trader lives in it from the day you claim it. Each level you evolve it to brings the next trader. A Renowned market has all four.

| Level | Trader | Trades in |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Grub | Rocks, wood and food |
| 1 | Snikkit | Weapons, armour and ores |
| 2 | Mottle | Glass, brick and finery |
| 3 | Bramble | Seeds, eggs and wool |

Point at a trader to open their stall.

## City merchants

Living cities have two more, at stalls in the plaza. They are people, not goblins.

| Name | Trades in |
|---|---|
| Ottavio | Fine goods from far away |
| Mabel | Food, drink and seed |
