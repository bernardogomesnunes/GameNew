# Time travel

At Age 11, make a **Time Machine** at an Engineering Centre from 24 Iron Ingots, 8 Gold, 10 Glass, 2 Diamonds and 2 Rubies. Place it, then use it to choose **To the Past** or **To the Future**. Both destinations have a return machine near arrival. Use that machine to return to the position you left.

## The Past

The Past has jungle and fern plains with dinosaurs and nests. Raptors hunt; Triceratops defend themselves. Tame Raptors with Raw Meat, and Triceratops or Long-necks with Fruit or Carrots. Place on a tamed animal to ride.

Collect eggs and bring them home. Place an egg and wait **two game days**: it hatches into a tame companion. Pick it back up before hatching to move it. Craft a **Dinosaur Whistle** and use Break to switch nearby companions between following and staying. Following dinosaurs fight nearby enemies. A companion left far away waits there until you return for it.

The jungle also has a rare **Basilisk**. It is hostile and cannot be tamed. Defeating it yields Basilisk Scales.

## The Future

Arrive in a city of glass towers, lit windows, neon, maglev trams and flying cars. Futurians walk around its central plaza. The university stands **east of the plaza**.

Find the university's **Archive** and use Read. You learn how to make the city's paving, asphalt, tinted glass, steel panels, neon, screens, maglev rail and lamps. Return home and craft those recipes at an Engineering Centre. Visiting the Future alone does not unlock them; reading the Archive does.

The city vehicles supply its traffic. The player's buildable flying vehicle is the [Helix](vehicles.md#helix); the Plainer is wearable gliding gear.

## The final age

Age 12 requires visiting the Past and hatching a dinosaur, alongside opening the Sanctuary's route and defeating the other god or taking his stash. See [Goals](goals.md).
