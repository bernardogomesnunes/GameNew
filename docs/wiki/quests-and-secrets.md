# Quests, secrets and the Collection Book

Beyond the age goals, the game has extra things to do. None of them blocks your progress.

## Quests

Errands from the hermit, messengers, your settlers and Grub the trader.

- Quests are optional. No age, building or recipe ever waits on one.
- No quest needs another.
- Each opens at its age and stays open until you finish it.
- Hand them in from the Goals panel, wherever you are. The person who asked does not need to be nearby.
- Counting quests (hunt, buy, evolve) count from the moment the quest opens.

### What they say

- **A bare orchard.** "My trees have gone bare this year. If your forest gives, send me six fruit, and I'll send you seeds for a garden." *Seeds come back wrapped in a leaf, with a note: "Lettuce and cabbage. Put one of each in your farm and it'll grow them for you."*
- **Meat for the winter.** A passing messenger looks you over: "There's deer and boar in these woods. Hunt three, and you won't go hungry this winter." *"There, the land feeds whoever works it. Cook the meat before you eat it."*
- **Before the first winter.** "I fell too, once. The first nights down here are the coldest. Build four walls and a roof before anything else." *"Good. A roof is the first thing the sky can't take from you. Keep it.", the hermit*
- **Mud in the doorways.** "Mud in the doorways, mud in the bread. Bring us thirty-two cobblestones and we'll lay a proper yard." *"The yard's laid. You can hear boots on stone now, it sounds like a town."*
- **The hermit is unwell.** "The old hermit is too sick to hunt. Send five cooked meats, and he says he'll owe you a story." *The messenger comes back: "He ate, and he talked. He says the island you fell from hangs on four chains, and the chains are older than either kingdom. He sent coffee seed, too."*
- **Eggs without chasing.** "If we had animals penned, we'd have eggs and wool without running after them. Fence a pen and lead some in." *"The children have named every one of them. Here, potato seed, from a sack we carried all the way here."*
- **A dark hut.** "In the Sky Kingdom every window caught the light. Make me eight panes of glass, my hut has been dark for twenty years." *"Light again. I'd forgotten how it falls through glass in the morning. Take these lanterns, I've no use for them now."*
- **First customer.** Grub the goblin leans over his counter: "Market's open, chief. First customer gets a coin back for luck. Buy anything." *"Pleasure doing business. Come back when your purse is heavier, I've cousins who sell steel."*
- **Somewhere to sit at night.** "A town needs a place to sit at night and hear the news. Raise a tavern and we'll fill it." *"Full every night. The talk is all of the Stone Kingdom, and whether it will come for us."*
- **Iron of your own.** "The Stone Kingdom smelts its own iron. Whether you mean to stand against it or beside it, you'll want a foundry of your own." *"Iron of your own. Here are four ingots from a friend in the hills, to start you off."*
- **The white god's lights.** "They say the white god's fireflies came down with me. Catch two jars of them on a warm night, and let me see them once more." *"The same ones. The same green. Here, take this to your temple. It's more yours than mine."*
- **A hearth in every house.** "The workshop smokes and the kiln roars, let's have something to show for it. Twenty-four bricks, for a proper hearth in every house." *"Warm hearths. Folk stay up later now, telling stories about where you came from."*
- **Worth walking past.** "Your buildings could do more, chief. Give one what its next level asks for, lights, a chest, and evolve it." *"Look at it now. Folk walk out of their way to see it, and walk past my stall on the way."*
- **Below the easy ground.** "Everything easy is above ground, chief. Let's dig a proper mine." *"Down there it's dark and cool, and the rock rings when you strike it."*
- **Before they test you.** "Bandits test every new town. Raise a stretch of stone wall, with battlements, before they test yours." *"They'll think twice now. So will others."*
- **Older than kingdoms.** "With the wall down you can go where you like. There are ruins out there from before either kingdom, find one." *"So you've seen them. Whoever built those walls built them to last, and still they fell."*
- **Come yourself.** "You've sent me fruit and glass and fireflies. Now come yourself, my hut is out past the hills." *The hermit takes your hand: "You still have the Sky Kingdom's look about you. Go well, and don't spend too long looking up."*
- **Where every road ends.** "Every old road ends at the Stone Kingdom. See it for yourself, the dark walls, the red tower." *"Now you know what's out there, and who you might stand against, or beside."*

## Daily challenges

Short building challenges that rotate by date, so everyone sees the same ones on the same day. Find them under Today in the Goals panel.


| Challenge | Reward |
|---|---|
| Build something using only 3 block types. | 60 XP |
| Build a structure taller than 10 blocks. | 60 XP, unlocks a block early |
| Spread a build across a 10x10 or larger footprint. | 60 XP |
| Place 100 blocks in one session. | 80 XP |
| Use at least 5 glass blocks in a build. | 50 XP, unlocks a block early |
| Place 6 different block types without repeating in a row. | 70 XP |
| Place 10+ blocks while symmetry mode is active. | 55 XP |
| Break 20 blocks underground. | 55 XP |
