# Goals

The game is a ladder of twelve **ages**. Each age has a few goals. Finish every goal and your land grows, new buildings and recipes open up, and the next age begins.

- Ages 1 to 5 have three goals each. Ages 6 to 12 have four.
- Every goal counts. Goals from later ages stay locked until you reach them.
- You cannot walk past your border until **Age 10**, when the wall comes down and the Stone Kingdom declares war.
- The game cannot be finished while the war is on, and on the dark path it cannot be finished until the Sky Kingdom has fallen.
- Open the Goals panel with **G** to see your current goals, with progress.

## Every goal


### Age 1: Exile

*Thrown down from the sky with nothing but an axe and a bucket. Chop a tree, fetch water, and make yourself a pickaxe.*

Land: **32 by 32 blocks**.

| Goal | How |
|---|---|
| Break a tree for wood | Swing your axe at a tree trunk. The wood goes into your bag. |
| Fill your bucket with water | Hold your bucket and tap water to fill it. |
| Make a pickaxe | Open your bag and make a stone pickaxe, dig stone out of the ground, or knock it out of cobblestone. |

### Age 2: Roots

*There is no climbing back. Claim a forest, break ground on a farm, and build somewhere to sleep.*

Land: **64 by 64 blocks**.

| Goal | How |
|---|---|
| Plant and claim a forest | Standing trees that keep giving, instead of a stump you cut once. |
| Break ground on a farm | Turned soil beside fresh water. Put a seed in for each crop you want, up to four, and it grows them. |
| Build yourself a house | Somewhere to sleep. Later, somewhere for a settler to sleep. |

### Age 3: Settle

*Put roots down: open a quarry, raise a storehouse, and a second house, someone is coming.*

Land: **96 by 96 blocks**.

| Goal | How |
|---|---|
| Open a quarry | A hole you dig into rock, open to the sky. |
| Raise a storehouse, your bag is not big enough | Shelves that are not your back. Build it bigger and it holds more. |
| Build a second house, somebody moves in | Somewhere to sleep. Later, somewhere for a settler to sleep. |

### Age 4: Forge

*Stone and fire: a workshop for what your hands cannot make, a kiln for brick and glass, and animals of your own.*

Land: **128 by 128 blocks**.

| Goal | How |
|---|---|
| Build a workshop | A room with a bench in it. Stand here and you can make what your hands cannot. |
| Fire up a kiln | Earth and sand go in. Brick and glass come out. |
| Keep animals in a pen | A fence with a gate, and animals inside it. Lead them in with food in your hand, then shut the gate. |

### Age 5: Hearth

*Enough people to be a place. Raise a market, a tavern and a foundry, and hear travellers' news of the two kingdoms.*

Land: **160 by 160 blocks**.

| Goal | How |
|---|---|
| Raise a market | Somewhere the things your buildings make change hands. |
| Raise a tavern | Somewhere to eat, drink and hear the news. Small coin changes hands too. |
| Raise a foundry | A hotter fire than the kiln keeps, hot enough to run ore. |

### Age 6: Town

*A town needs a hall, a place of learning, and homes for more than one family. Look after what you have built.*

Land: **192 by 192 blocks**.

| Goal | How |
|---|---|
| Raise a townhouse, three households under one roof | More roof than one family needs, so more than one family lives under it. |
| Found a university | Desks under a roof. Study here to get better at what you do, and to learn engineering. |
| Raise a town hall | Where the town keeps its accounts. Comes with a storage controller that reaches every storehouse. |
| Evolve a building a level | Give a building what its next level asks for, lights, a chest, and press Evolve in its pop-up. |

### Age 7: Faith

*Deep down, Sunstone and Nightstone wait. Raise a shrine, build it up, and at its height choose your god.*

Land: **224 by 224 blocks**.

| Goal | How |
|---|---|
| Raise a shrine | Somewhere to pray. Offerings and worshippers turn into devotion, and devotion into blessings. |
| Raise an engineering centre | A long hall with a wide door. Where carts, flying machines and trains will be worked out. |
| Fill a granary | A dry room full of what the farms grew, so a bad season is not a crisis. |
| Forge a ring, choose your god | Build your shrine up to its height and forge the White Ring or the Black. Which one is your choice. |

### Age 8: Bastion

*The Stone Kingdom has heard of you. Drive a mine, raise a garrison and a barracks, and your god's Sanctuary.*

Land: **256 by 256 blocks**.

| Goal | How |
|---|---|
| Drive a mine underground | Everything easy is above ground. This is not above ground. |
| Raise a garrison | Walls with people behind them. Nothing has tested them yet, and that is rather the point. |
| Raise a barracks | A hall of bunks with arms on the walls and a yard to train in. Train warriors, swordsmen, archers and catapult crews here, a soldier a bunk, for food and gear, they go out to meet the Stone Kingdom's army. |
| Build your Sanctuary | The White Sanctuary or the Black, the one for the ring you bear. Its guardian comes to you. |

### Age 9: Walls

*Soon the wall comes down, and they will come over it. Raise walls, a gate and a watchtower while there is time.*

Land: **320 by 320 blocks**.

| Goal | How |
|---|---|
| Raise a stone wall | A length of wall with a walk along the top and battlements to fight from. Claimed, it takes three blows a block to break. |
| Raise a gatehouse | Two towers and a gate between them, with a walk across the top. It shuts its gate when a round of the war is coming. |
| Raise a watchtower | A tall tower with a lookout on top. Two archers keep watch from it and shoot at anything of the Stone Kingdom's that comes in range. |
| Fifteen buildings standing | A settlement that would show up on a map. |

### Age 10: Reckoning

*The wall is down, and the Stone Kingdom is coming. Hold your land through ten rounds, then raise a village and a monument.*

Land: **384 by 384 blocks** (open: the border no longer stops you walking).

| Goal | How |
|---|---|
| End the war, win it, or bring down the Sky Kingdom | Beat the ten rounds of the Stone Kingdom, or, sworn to the dark god, bring down the Sky Kingdom. |
| Raise a village | A forest, a farm, housing and a shed, folded into one claim, everything a start needs, raised at once. |
| Raise a monument | Nothing useful. That is the point of it. |
| Take a building to Renowned | Take any building to the top of its ladder. |

On the dark path this age reads: *The wall is down. The Stone King calls you his own, and the Sky Kingdom doesn't know you're coming. Raise a village, and take back the sky.*

### Age 11: Renown

*The world past your border is yours to walk. Go and find what the old peoples left behind.*

Land: **448 by 448 blocks** (open: the border no longer stops you walking).

| Goal | How |
|---|---|
| Find a ruin | Old walls standing in the grass. Messengers and explorers say where. |
| Find a forgotten temple | Marble pillars, half of them fallen, and an altar. |
| Find an abandoned mine | Timber over a hole in the ground, left in a hurry. |
| Find a monument | A tall black stone, gold at the top. |

### Age 12: Legend

*They tell stories about you now. Make the place worth the stories.*

Land: **512 by 512 blocks** (open: the border no longer stops you walking).

| Goal | How |
|---|---|
| A market town, all four traders | Evolve a market until all four traders live in it, stone, steel, glass and seed. |
| Hold a hundred coins | Strike them at a foundry, sell to the traders, or find them in old chests. |
| Twenty-five buildings standing | A town people walk days to see. |
| Wear armour on head, body, legs and feet | Anything on all four: leather, iron, gold, made at the bench. |

This is the last age. Finishing it finishes the game.


## Notes on particular goals

- **Evolve a building a level (Age 6):** give any building what its next level asks for (usually a lamp), then press Evolve in its pop-up. The "Craft what's missing" button makes the lamp for you.
- **Forge a ring (Age 7):** build the Temple up to the High Temple, then forge the White Ring or the Black Ring. This is a one-way choice. See [Lore and story](lore.md).
- **End the war (Age 10):** on the white path, win the Ten Rounds. On the dark path, bring down the Sky Kingdom. See [Combat and war](combat-and-war.md).
- **Take a building to Renowned (Age 10):** the top level of any building (Renowned, or the High Temple).
- **A market town (Age 12):** evolve a market to level 3 so all four traders live in it.
- **Hold 100 coins (Age 12):** strike coins at a foundry, sell to traders, or find them in chests.
- **Armour on head, body, legs and feet (Age 12):** any armour counts, even leather.
