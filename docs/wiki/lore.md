# Lore and story

## The world in brief

Two kingdoms and two gods.

- The **Sky Kingdom** is a floating island of white halls and gold roofs, lit by fireflies all night and held in the sky on four great chains. Its people follow the **white god**, who has no name. You were born there.
- The **Stone Kingdom** is a walled city of dark stone. Its people follow the **dark god**, who has more names than anyone can count. Its army is why you fell. It has wanted the sky for longer than anyone remembers.
- The **Stone King** wants the sky. The **Sky King** will not let go of it. Everything in the land turns on the two of them.

You fell from the Sky Kingdom the day the Stone army came to its chains. You woke far from anywhere, with 32 blocks of land, an axe and a bucket.

## The choice

At the top of the Temple you forge **one ring**. That choice is final for that world, and decides how the game ends. You see the other path in another world.

| | White path: stay true to the sky | Dark path: turn to stone |
|---|---|---|
| Ring | White Ring: 1.2 times faster, 1.3 times higher jump | Black Ring: sparks back at whoever hits you |
| Ring cost | 3 Sunstone, 4 Gold, 12 Devotion | 3 Nightstone, 4 Obsidian, 12 Devotion |
| Sanctuary | White Sanctuary (marble, light, open sky) | Black Sanctuary (obsidian around a pit) |
| Guardian | Aurelion, the white stag | Umbra, the shadow beast |
| The Stone Kingdom | Your enemy | Your ally |
| The Sky Kingdom | Never appears | Your target |
| How you win | Beat the Ten Rounds of the Stone army | Take the Sky Kingdom with 1,000 warriors |
| Your ending | The dark army broken, fireflies bless your land | Lord of the Sky |

If you forge neither ring, the war still comes at Age 10 and you must still beat it. Forging the Black Ring during the war calls the war off.

## The gods

**The white god.** The god of the sky folk, who has no name. They never needed one. The White Ring is his gift: speed, and a higher jump. The Stone Kingdom will come for it, round after round.

**The dark god.** The Stone Kingdom's god. The Black Ring is his: a spark that hurts whoever hits you. If you bear it, the Stone King calls you his own.

## The Temple and devotion

- Build a Temple (unlocks at Age 4). It climbs Shrine, Chapel, Temple, Great Temple, High Temple.
- Each level is built (windows, lights, pillars, gold trim, banners) and then paid for in **devotion** (3, 8, 15 and 25).
- The Temple makes devotion over time, more with worshipping settlers.
- Offerings add devotion: fruit (6), a harvest (4 carrots), a meal (2 cooked meat), gold (the best), or a lit candle.
- Holy Water (1 Glass, 1 Devotion) heals four hearts. It needs a Chapel.
- At the High Temple you can forge a ring. Sunstone and Nightstone are the hunt: very rare crystals on deep cave walls and in chests.

## The Sanctuary and the guardian

Only the bearer of a ring can raise its Sanctuary (10 devotion). Raising it calls your god's guardian, who follows you, fights bandits, and keeps you as long as the Sanctuary stands.

| | White Sanctuary | Black Sanctuary |
|---|---|---|
| Built from | 30 marble, sky marble or gold trim, 4 lights, open sky | 30 obsidian, dark stone or dark brick, a pit at its heart |
| Guardian | Aurelion | Umbra |

## The Stone Kingdom

A walled city of dark stone 1,000 to 1,400 blocks from home. An old road always leads to its gate.

- Walls ten high with seven towers, and a gatehouse with black banners and a gold lintel.
- Streets of calçada with lamp posts.
- The keep, with pillars, a red carpet and the throne.
- The dark god's temple, with an altar of Nightstone.
- An armoury, barracks, a market of calçada waves with stalls, and about twenty dark houses.
- The Stone King stands on his dais and speaks to you according to your ring when you tap him.
- Guards at nine posts are hostile to the White Ring, and to anyone who strikes one.

## The Sky Kingdom

A floating island about 5,000 blocks from home, 220 blocks across. It exists only on the dark path (and in Creative, to look at). It leaves a hole in the ground below it, as if torn out of the land. A small gold light shows low in the sky in its direction, brighter at night.

- Rock underneath, deepest in the middle, with three waterfalls off the rim and white trees.
- A white city wall with a gold band, eight towers and a gatehouse on each of four avenues.
- Four wide calçada avenues to the citadel, lamps down both sides.
- Three districts of houses (cottages, two-storey townhouses, villas with porticos), with gardens and fountain squares.
- A military quarter: barracks, armouries and training yards.
- The citadel: its own wall and corner towers, a courtyard with trees and fountains, and the palace with a stepped gold roof and spire.
- The throne room: a blue rug, firefly lamps on marble pillars, and the Sky King on his golden throne.
- About thirty guards in white and gold, and two royal guards always at the King's side.
- Four **anchor towers** on the ground around it. Each is sky marble with gold corners, a stair winding up inside, a platform with lamps, and a chain up to the island's rim. Two guards stand at the door and one on the platform.
- A **Sky Lift** at the top of each tower rides up its chain to the island. A lift at each landing outside the gates rides back down. Warriors following you come too.

## The white path: the Ten Rounds

At Age 10 the Stone Kingdom attacks. Ten rounds, each bigger, ending with the Warlord. See [Combat and war](combat-and-war.md) for each round.

- Your White Sanctuary and Aurelion fight beside you.
- Win round 10 and the raids end for good. The white god's light settles on your land with fireflies across your settlement at night.
- **Ending:** the dark army in retreat from your walls, the fireflies' blessing on your village, your own kingdom at dawn. "Ten times the Stone Kingdom came for you, and ten times it went home with nothing."

## The dark path: take the sky

Forge the Black Ring and the Stone King becomes your ally.

1. **Swear the oath.** In the Stone Kingdom's dark temple, with the Black Ring on, Place on the Nightstone heart of the altar. The King grants you 1,000 warriors.
2. **March.** The Sky Kingdom is about 5,000 blocks away: 15 to 20 minutes on foot. Pitch camp, or send the army ahead as an expedition.
3. **Reach the island.** Take an anchor tower and ride its lift up.
4. **Take the Sky Kingdom** one of three ways:

**Storm it.** Fight across the island with warriors, catapults, archers and Umbra, and take the palace.

**Go home in disguise.** In the full Sky armour you walk in as one of them. A **suspicion meter** (an eye and a bar) fills while you run, show the Black Ring or Stone Kingdom gear, crowd a guard, linger in the throne room, or bring more than three warriors. It drains while you behave. A ? shows over guards looking you over and a ! over those who have seen through you. When it fills, or you strike, you are discovered and it is open battle. Your first blow while unseen lands three times as hard.

**Cut the chains.** Break any link of a tower's chain to cut the whole chain. It drops away and that tower's lift stops working. Cut all four and the island sinks. You are told each day, and two game days later the Sky Kingdom yields and the King comes down without a blow.

**The throne room fight.** The Sky King raises his arms and glows white before a heavy blow. Step back and it misses. If a royal guard falls while the King lives, another comes from the palace door 18 seconds later.

**If you lose an attack.** An attack begins when you reach the island or its towers with the Black Ring and ends if you leave alive. You lose it if you fall there or your whole army does. You wake at home, what you carried is left in a chest where you fell, and the Sky Kingdom taxes you 25% of what your buildings make, then 35%, 45%, and at most 50% after each lost attack, until it falls. Every building that makes something shows a Taxes line. The Stone King makes up 75% of the warriors lost, then 50%, then 25%, then none.

**If you win.** When the Sky King falls, its guards lay down their arms. The island becomes your land and you can build anywhere on it. The Stone King names you **Lord of the Sky** and sends 64 gold.

- **Ending (by force):** "You struck the Sky King down in his own throne room. The Stone King named you Lord of the Sky." His empty throne and fallen crown, the island under black banners, and you as Lord of the Sky.
- **Ending (by chains):** "You cut the four chains, and the island sank until its King came down and gave it up." The island sinking, its King coming down, and you as Lord of the Sky.

## The lore book

The Lore tab in the Goals panel fills in as you learn. It has eleven pages, each locked until you hear it, find it or do it.

| Page | Opens when |
|---|---|
| The fall | From the start |
| The Sky Kingdom | You hear of the light in the sky, or bear the Black Ring |
| The Stone Kingdom | You hear of it, find it, or the war starts |
| The two gods | You hear of them, or raise a temple |
| The white god | You hear of him, or forge the White Ring |
| The dark god | You hear of him, or forge the Black Ring |
| The two kings | You hear of them, or find the Stone Kingdom |
| The hermit | You talk to the hermit |
| Your guardian | You raise a Sanctuary, or hear of guardians |
| The four chains | You hear of the towers, or swear the oath |
| How it ended | Your path ends |

The first and last pages can replay the opening and the ending.

## The story told on screen

- A new Duilt world starts with a four-page illustrated intro: the Sky Kingdom among its fireflies, the Stone army at its chains, the fall, and your arrival.
- Each path ends with its own illustrated ending.
