# Creatures and people

## Animals

Animals roam the wild. Hunt them for meat and hides, or keep some in a [Pen](buildings.md).

| Animal | Where | Health | Drops | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Deer | Forests, plains, giant groves | 6 | 2 to 3 raw meat, 1 to 2 hide | Bolts when you come near |
| Rabbit | Plains, desert, birch forest | 2 | 1 raw meat, 0 to 1 hide | Bolts |
| Boar | Dark forest, oak forest, groves | 9 | 2 to 4 raw meat, 1 to 2 hide | Runs only once hit |
| Goat | Mountains, high peaks | 6 | 1 to 2 raw meat, 1 hide | Bolts |
| Sheep | Plains, mountains | 5 | 1 to 2 raw meat, 1 to 3 wool | Can be penned |
| Cow | Plains, wetland | 9 | 3 to 4 raw meat, 1 to 2 hide | Can be penned |
| Pig | Plains, oak forest, wetland | 5 | 2 to 3 raw meat | Can be penned |
| Chicken | Plains, oak forest, wetland | 2 | 1 raw meat, 1 to 3 feather | Can be penned |
| Horse | Plains | 12 | 2 to 3 raw meat, 1 to 2 hide | Bolts. Tame it, then ride it |

**Keeping animals:** fence in an area with a gate, hold food, lead them in, and shut the gate. A claimed Pen makes meat and its animals’ eggs, milk or wool. The Pen is an Age 4 goal.

**Taming a horse:** hold fruit or vegetables and Place on it. A tamed horse can be ridden and can pull a cart. See [Vehicles](vehicles.md).

**Fireflies:** swarms come out at night. Make a Glass Jar, then Break on a swarm to catch them.

## Your people

| Who | What they do |
|---|---|
| Settlers | Move into houses, staff producers (+50% output), or take Builder and Hauler jobs at a Work Hall. Eat one food a day |

## Neutral unless you steal

Tree people and city folk ignore you. Strike one, or take from a house's chest, and the guards come for you while everyone else runs.

## Dinosaurs and the Future

The [Past](time-travel.md) has Raptors, Triceratops and Long-necks. Tame a Raptor with Raw Meat; tame the herbivores with Fruit or Carrots. Place on a tamed dinosaur to ride. A Dinosaur Whistle switches nearby companions between following and staying, and following companions fight nearby threats. Eggs set down by the player hatch tame after two game days.

The Basilisk is a dangerous, untameable jungle creature that drops Basilisk Scales. Futurians live around the Future's arrival plaza and talk about their city and university. Once you have read the Archive, their guidance acknowledges that knowledge.
