# Vehicles

## Horse and cart

- Horses live wild on the plains and bolt when you come near.
- Tame one: hold fruit or vegetables and Place on it. A tamed horse can be ridden.
- Make a **Cart** by hand: 16 Planks, 4 Wood (Age 3). Use it on a tamed horse and the horse pulls it.
- A cart carries 40 slots, and you can ride it.

## Plainer

Make a Plainer at a Workshop from 24 Planks and 12 Wool (Age 4). Equip it in the Body slot, climb somewhere high and step off. It glides down and across; it does not hover or gain height with Fly. Take it off to wear body armour instead.

## Helix

Make a Helix at an Engineering Centre from 20 Iron Ingots, 8 Glass and 6 Planks (Age 8). Place it on the ground, then Place on it to climb in. It can hover and fly: Jump rises, Sneak descends. Land, then Sneak to get out.

## Trains

A train runs on a line of **rail** and burns coal. Each part is 8 blocks long, 3 wide and 4 high.

| Part | Made at | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Rail ×8 | Engineering Centre (Age 8) | 1 Iron Ingot, 2 Planks |
| Steam Engine | Engineering Centre (Age 8) | 24 Iron Ingot, 8 Brick, 8 Planks |
| Rail Car | Engineering Centre (Age 8) | 12 Iron Ingot, 12 Planks |

You need to build an Engineering Centre first, and study engineering at a University to unlock it.

**Lay the line.** Rail joins its neighbours, turns corners and climbs one step by itself. The engine needs at least 9 rail in a straight run.

**Set the engine down.** Hold the Steam Engine and Place on a rail. It faces the way you look, and needs 8 rail behind it.

**Fuel.** Hold coal and Place on the engine. The bunker holds 64. One coal carries it 120 blocks, and only while you are driving; coasting is free. You get coal from:

- coal ore in the High Peaks;
- a Mine's output;
- the Foundry: "Burn charcoal" turns 4 Wood into 1 Coal.

**Drive.** Place on the engine to climb into the cab. Move forward and back to drive. Top speed is 12 blocks a second. Let go and it rolls to a stop. Sneak to get down.

**Add cars.** Hold a Rail Car and Place on a train to couple it on the back. An engine pulls up to five.

**Cargo.** Each car holds 1,000 slots of goods. Place on a car to open it (200 slots a page). A car with goods in it stays coupled until you empty it.

**Passengers.** Each car has 10 seats. Stations are your settlement and every living city. Stop the engine within 40 blocks of a station: whoever was going there gets off and pays 1 coin for every 120 blocks they rode, and new passengers get on for another station. They sit in the windows. Point at a car to see who is aboard and where they are going.

**Take it apart.** Hit the train to take the last car off, then the engine (with its coal) back into your bag. A car with goods in it must be emptied first.

In Creative a train needs no engine item and its fire never goes out.

## The Sky Lift

The Sky Lift is a golden winch: 2 Gold, 2 Chain, 2 Planks, made by hand at Age 10. A Chain is 1 Iron Ingot for 4, at Age 5.

It carries you only where the world already put one: the lifts at the top of the four anchor towers, and the lifts at the landings by the Sky Kingdom's gates. A Sky Lift you place anywhere else does nothing when you Ride it.

- Aim at a lift and Ride to travel along its chain.
- A tower's lift stops working once its chain is cut.
- In Creative the tower lifts always work.
